## Our story

NES was founded in 2020 by a group of friends with a simple idea: to make our area a livelier, more welcoming place. We have grown since then and today we have dozens of members and volunteers.

## What we believe in

- **Participation**: everyone can contribute, in small ways or big ones.
- **Culture**: learning and sharing make us richer.
- **Solidarity**: nobody should be left behind.
