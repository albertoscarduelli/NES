## Why join

Being a member of NES means having a say in decisions, access to all our initiatives and, above all, being part of a group of people who want to do good.

## How to join, in three steps

1. **Get in touch** by email, or come and meet us at one of our events.
2. **Fill in the membership form** we will give you.
3. **Pay the annual fee** of €20 (€10 for under-18s).

## Want to help without becoming a member?

We are always looking for volunteers for our events: even a few hours make a difference. Write to us!
